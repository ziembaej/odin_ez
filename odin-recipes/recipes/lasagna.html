Lasagna Recipe

Here is a delicious lasagna recipe to try at home.

Ingredients:

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Egg
  • Garlic
  • Onion
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  3. In a skillet, cook the ground beef until browned. Drain excess fat.
  4. Add the tomato sauce to the cooked beef and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. In a bowl, mix the ricotta cheese, egg, and a pinch of salt and pepper.
  6. In a baking dish, layer noodles, meat sauce, ricotta mixture, and mozzarella cheese. Repeat layers.
  7. Top with Parmesan cheese.
  8. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake for an additional 25 minutes until cheese is golden and bubbly.
  9. Let the lasagna rest for 10 minutes before serving.
