Burger Recipe

Here is a delicious burger recipe to try at home.

Ingredients:

  • Ground beef
  • Burger buns
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Cheese
  • Onion
  • Pickles
  • Ketchup
  • Mustard
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the grill or a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Season the ground beef with salt and pepper and form into patties.
  3. Cook the patties for 4-5 minutes per side, or until desired doneness.
  4. Toast the burger buns on the grill or skillet.
  5. Assemble the burgers with lettuce, tomato, cheese, onion, pickles, and condiments of choice.
  6. Serve immediately and enjoy!
